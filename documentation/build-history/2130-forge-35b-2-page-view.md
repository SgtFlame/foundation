Page view for the rich-text editor's `page` mode: page setup and headers and footers in the doc node's attrs (kept in the room as the Y.Map `page` and a fragment per header and footer, since y-prosemirror syncs no doc attrs), pagination as decorations from a pure, tested paginator, page and section breaks, a live table of contents, page numbers, indents with a ruler, footnotes at the foot of their page, and print through a frame. Later parts: a node a document may hold still goes in `richTextSchema()` and, when it has text, in `text/prosemirror`; anything kept in the doc's attrs must be kept in the room beside the fragment as `content.ts` does, or it is lost on the first save.
