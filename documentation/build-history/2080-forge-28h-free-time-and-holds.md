## Forge 28h: free time, holds and the scheduling tools
Built 2026-11-27 ([Calendar.md](Calendar.md) › *Built in forge 28h*). The `calendar` library app gained the tools `findFreeTime` (the caller and the people named, over the calendars each **owns** and their `calAvailability`, through *Free slots*), `hold`, `choose`, `release`, `proposeMeeting` (an agent with `suggest` autonomy gets slots only) and `respond`, the `calHold` type with tentative events `<hold>_s<n>`, and the hourly trigger `onHoldsExpire`. Later parts: free time ignores team calendars (no `owner`); *New id* differs per *For each* iteration, so a held event finds its hold by its own id (`split(item.event, "_s")[0]`); the review card for a proposal is 28i's.
