For Forge's Tasks app, which shows the Tasks library app's records: an editor kind's subject may be a required app's entity type (presence on `record_<type>.<id>`, the `record` behind its templates), and the document check accepts `<requiredApp>.<query>` names in prefabs. Later apps built over a library app (CRM over Tasks, Docs databases) get their tabs' subjects and queries this way, with no copies of the library's types.
