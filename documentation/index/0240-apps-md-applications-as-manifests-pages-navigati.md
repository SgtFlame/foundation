and a draft run for its authors (`<appId>/draft`, prefabs and graphs in their drafts, `draft.<appId>.<name>` commands and queries sent for real); and the operation log: every change to a definition an op with its request, actor and inverse, read by `ops.list` and replayed into another tenant; and undo and redo by request (`ops.undo`, `ops.redo`, `ops.stack`), with restorable deletes for prefabs, graphs and apps; and the chat's read tools over the catalog and an app (`forge.catalog`, `forge.describe`, `forge.app`, `forge.read`), with every catalog item's and command's metadata; and the chat's tools that change and check an app: `forge.apply` (one atomic batch, others' changes reported), `forge.changes`, `forge.check` (the draft as it would be exported, with warnings), `forge.preview` (a page as a manifest role) and `forge.undo`; and `forge.test`, a blueprint run in a sandbox whose commands are decided but never committed |
