**Games** (forge 300 to 390, `documentation/Game.md`): the engine is `frontend/engine` (`@foundation/engine`, a third Yarn workspace consumed as source; three.js WebGPU with a WebGL 2 fallback, smooth voxels meshed in workers); game definitions are `features/game` (`game.*` ops on the `def` area, worlds as `gameWorld`), and game rules are blueprints (`cmd-game-editTerrain`), with play, such as terrain edits, on the `game` area and not ops.
