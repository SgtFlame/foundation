**Boards** (forge 30b, `documentation/ServerUI.md` › *Built in forge 30b*): `mui.kanban` draws columns of server-rendered cards (`as`, keyed `<board>_<id>`, sending `placed` rather than records) and only reports drops (`move {id, from, to, index, after, before}`, which a command turns into `record.move`); the browser holds a drop until the card's `_seq` changes or 5 s pass, so the later move wins on every screen.
