**Calendar** (`features/calendar`, forge 28, `documentation/Calendar.md`): zoned times, recurrence and free slots are `calendar/when` (the tz database embedded; expand in the series' zone, gap forward, overlap earlier) behind the pure `time.*` nodes; `.ics` is parsed only in a child (`ical.Child`, `FOUNDATION_ICAL_CHILD`, checked first in `cli/main.go`) and composed in process; `calendar.events@1` is the one contract for outside calendars, and every type passes `calendar/contracttest` (the `caltest.calendar` test server, scripted by `caltest.control@1` and the `caltest.call` live call, does in CI).
