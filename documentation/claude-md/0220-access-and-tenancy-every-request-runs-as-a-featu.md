**Access and tenancy** - Every request runs as a `features.Principal` (`{Kind, UserID, TenantID}`), set by `rest.Middleware(rest.WithAuthenticate(auth.Authenticate))` from the `foundation_access` cookie (`identity.Authenticator`, see *Identity* below). While no user exists (single-user mode) it is `features.LocalPrincipal()` (reserved IDs: tenant 1, user 1, who holds *Super Administrator*); afterwards a request without a valid session is `features.AnonymousPrincipal()`, and `rest.RequireUser()` (on every route group except `/identity`) refuses it with 401 `session_expired` or `unauthenticated`. Background work uses `features.BackgroundArgs(ctx)` (a service principal). Every new operation calls `authz.Check(args.Context(), args.Principal, "<type>.<action>", resource).Err(action, resource)` first and fails with `authz.Status` (403); the action must be declared (see *Roles* below) or it is denied. Resource paths are `app`, `tenant:<id>`, and paths under a tenant built with `authz.Tenant(p)` and `authz.Resource(kind, id)` (`tenant:1/volume:42`, `tenant:1/user:2/chronicles`); a user's own scope is `user:<id>/…`. Volumes, their index and collaboration belong to the default tenant until each tenant has its own knowledge base root: `kbhandlers.InTenant()` guards `/kb` and `/index` (403 `volumes_unavailable`), the `library` source and kb tools check `kbservice.InTenant`. Within the tenant, kb operations check `volume.read|write` on the Volume holding the folder, else `kb.read|write` (`volumeservice.Check`, `volumeservice.Resource`), and the `library` source drops chunks of Volumes the caller may not read. **Roles** (`features/authz`, see `documentation/RBAC.md` › *Built in slice 11*): a feature declares its resource types on `authz.ResourceTypes` (`authz.resourceType`) from a `ContributeAccess(reg)` that `serve.go` calls (`identity`, `config`, `logs`, `usage`, `conversation`, `chronicle`, `retrieval`, `cognition`, `kbservice`, `records`, `ui` with `ui.ContributePrefabAccess` (`prefabs`), `apps` (`app`); `authz.Contribute` adds `role`, `grant`, `secret`, `extensions`). Each `authz.Action` has a `Class` (`read`, `write`, `create`, `delete`, `share`, `moderate`, `execute`, `admin`, or `self`: no grant needed) and `On` (`app` or `tenant`, the default): pick these with care, since the built-in roles (reserved IDs 1-9: *Super Administrator*, *Tenant Administrator*, *Application Administrator*, *Global Moderator* on `app`; *Owner*, *Administrator*, *Moderator*, *Editor*, *Viewer* in a tenant) take actions by class. A type with `Owned` is the principal's when the path names `user:<their id>`. `serve.go` installs `authz.NewPolicy(appDB, registry)` with `authz.Install` after `identity.MigrateRoles`; `Check` then allows a user when a `RoleGrant` on the path or an ancestor holds the action (allow-only, with optional expiry), and before a policy is installed (other features' tests) allows everyone but anonymous principals. Grants, `CustomRole`s (per tenant) and `AccessAudit` live in the application database; change them through `Policy.Grant`/`Revoke`/`RevokeUnder` (inside a transaction, then `Changed()`), and in identity through `identity.Grant`/`RevokeUnder`/`Changed`. Granting needs `grant.write` and every permission of the role (`Policy.MayGrant`). New members get *Editor* (`identity.DefaultMemberRole`), a tenant's creator *Owner*, and leaving a tenant revokes its grants. **App roles** (slice 33, `documentation/Apps.md` › *Built in slice 33*): an installed app's roles are `CustomRole`s with `AppID`, made and removed only by the system (`Policy.SyncAppRoles`, `RemoveAppRoles`), read-only in `authzservice` (409 `app_role`); their permissions are scoped, `<scope>/<type>.<action>` (`app:tasks/entity:task/record.read`), held only on that path (`Role.HoldsOn`). A resource type with a `Fence` (`record`: `app`) is reached past a segment of that kind only by the Super Administrator, *Owner*, *Administrator* and that app's roles, so a tenant *Editor* does not see an installed app's records. `authz.RowRules(ctx, p, action, resource, key)` gives the row rules the allowing app roles put on a resource. A context made with `authz.WithAs(ctx, authz.As{Name, Covers, Rules})` decides the user checks its role covers, in `Check` and `RowRules`, by that role (slice 41: `forge.preview` as a draft app's role, `apptools.AsRole`), leaving the rest to the user's grants. `authzservice` (`/authz`: `GET me|resource-types|roles`, `POST roles/save|roles/delete|grants|grant|revoke|explain|agents`) serves the Role Editor, the Share dialog and the browser's `useCan`. **Agents** (slice 51, Forge 7j, `documentation/RBAC.md` › *Built in slice 51*): a principal of kind `agent` (`features.AgentPrincipal(tenant, agent, onBehalfOf)`) is a record of an entity type with a `principal` field (its id the record's, its name the title, its creator `_createdBy`, retired when the record is deleted), found through `authz.Agents` (`authz.SetAgents(recordsdata.Agents{…})` in `serve.go`; never mint or copy agents); it holds grants (`RoleGrant.AgentID`, `UserID` 0) and is checked against them alone, re-read after every change. `authz.AsAgent(ctx, p, id)` (its creator, or `agent.use` on its record) is how work runs as one: *Enqueue job* and *Call graph* take `As agent`, `jobs.Request.As` may be an agent; *Call graph*'s `reach` confines an agent's run to its subjects (`Principal.Reach`, forge 7p-3, `documentation/RBAC.md` › *Changed in forge 7p-3*: kept, never widened, granting nothing), which *Current user* gives as `reach` beside `kind` and `agent`, for a host's tools to refuse writes outside it, and *Current user*'s `agentRecord` is the agent's own defining record (an agent may always read its own settings, such as Mail's `autonomy`; RBAC.md › *Changed in forge 7p-4*); a manifest role's `agentWarning` is shown by the Share dialog when it is granted to an agent; an Agent node's run numbers its tools' passages `[n]` as a chat round does (Tools.md › *Changed in forge 7p-4*); its events carry `agentId`/`onBehalfOf` and the actor `agent:<id>`. **Memory and Volumes for agents** (forge 15a, `RBAC.md` › *Changed in forge 15a*): `Principal.MemoryOf` is whose Chronicle memory an agent's run may search, set only by *Call graph*'s `memoryOf` with `As agent` and checked by `authz.MayRemember` (a person names only themselves, an agent's run never widens it, an app any person), kept by `AsAgent` and `PrincipalRef`; the `chronicle` source answers such a run with that person's and the tenant's shared entries, never global ones. An action with `Agents` is held by every agent in its tenant (`retrieval.read`); one with `AsMaker` is decided by the grants of the person the run acts for, never the agent's own (`volume.read`). *Current user* also gives `memoryOf` and `owner` (the tenant's Owner). The Agent node keeps the passages it numbers on its context (`tools.Passages`), which *Run passages* (`tools.runPassages`) gives to the graphs of the tools it calls. **Since forge 15b** (`Tools.md` › *Changed in forge 15b*) `tools.Passages` lives in the run's `cognition.Scope` (made by the engine for a top-level run, inherited by the graphs it calls), so every model call of a run numbers on one list with chat's citer (`tools.Cite`, also the round citer's: a passage found again keeps its number); *Run passages* also gives `segments` in the round segment DTO shape. A grant's resource may be **one record** (`authz.RecordResource`: `…/entity:<type>/record:<id>`): for `record.*` it reaches that record of its type and, through the role's row rules, other types of its scope (`principal.hasRole(role, record)`, `authz.HasRole`); such grants always restrict `RowRules`. *Grant role*/*Revoke role* (`authz/nodes`) grant agents, or users (slice 56), as the run's principal through `MayGrant`, and an app's principal may grant its own roles on its own records, and *Grants* (`authz.grants`, forge 18a) lists the grants on an entity type's records (an app reads its own roles' on its own records); *Has role* (`authz.hasRole`) tells a graph, a command's too, whether its principal holds an app role on a record, and *May* (`authz.can`, forge 15b) whether it holds an action (for a page to show only what its viewer may use); row rules also see `principal.owner` (the tenant's Owner, `authz.IsOwner`), and a type's own access rule is the only one that restricts tenant Administrators; the `share` UI action (`Handle.Share`, the *Share* node) opens the Share dialog on a record. Since slice 54 (Forge 7m, `documentation/Apps.md` › *Changed in slice 54*) an app principal may use the agents its own entity types define, and holds actions marked `authz.Action.Apps` (`job.create`) in its tenant with no grant. Since slice 55 (Forge 7n, `documentation/Connectors.md` › *Built in slice 55*) an app principal may also use a connection a record of its own entity types holds in a `connection` field (`connections.Held`; check `use` with `Deps.CheckUse`, never `CheckAccess` alone, wherever an app may be the principal), and setting such a field checks its sender may use the connection (`records/data` `Deps.UseConnection`, wired in `serve.go`); a connector call's file reference `{id, heldBy: {entity, record, field}}` is vouched for by the dispatcher (`Dispatcher.Files` = `data.HeldFiles`) when the caller may read that record, and an adapter accepts it through `adapter.Context.Vouched`. Every new tenant-owned table embeds `db.Tenanted` after `db.Model`, and a user-owned one `db.Owned` too, filled from the principal (`db.TenantOf(p)`, `db.OwnedBy(p)`) and queried with `.Scopes(db.InTenant(p.TenantID))`.
