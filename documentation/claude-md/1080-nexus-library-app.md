**The Nexus library app** (forge 26d-3, `documentation/Nexus.md`): `features/nexus` holds native channels as app files (`app/`: entity types, command, query, projector and trigger graphs, tools), seeded by Forge's starter (`nexus.Files()`) or `nexus.Seed`; private channels are reached through the *Private channel member* role its `onMember` trigger grants per channel. An installed app's `record.read` query is now checked as the app's reach (`installs.reaches`), not on the tenant.
