- `src/chronicle/` - Chronicle in the browser: `chronicleStore.ts` (`useChronicles()`: entries, links and types, loaded once; `refreshChronicles()` after a change; `inputOf(entry)` for an update, from an entry read with `Chronicle.get`, since `list` leaves out the full summary and context), `chatFocus.ts` (the focused chat publishes the entries its last round chose and its memory update changed), `ContextView.tsx` (the **Context** side view, forge 16c: the workspace's Volumes and entries with their checkboxes) and `ChronicleControl.tsx` (the `chronicle` ⚙ control). The entry editor is `src/editors/chronicle/` (kind `chronicle`, `new ChronicleObject(entry)`; no entry is a new one).
