**Workbench context** (`src/workbench/context/`, see `documentation/WorkbenchContext.md` › *Built in slice 4*): `workbenchContext` (`WorkbenchContextStore.ts`) is this browser tab's record of the open editors, what each is editing, the focus order and the 20 most recent subjects (`viewed`, `edited`). `Workbench.tsx` feeds it the layout's tabs and focus through a flexlayout change listener (border tabs excluded); editors report what they edit with `useWorkbenchSubject(tabId, { type, id, label, summary?, details? }, { selection, dirty })` (`WorkbenchContext.ts`; `details`, more of what it shows by name, forge 7p-6: an installed app's editor kind fills them from its manifest's `subject.details` templates over the record, params and the page prefab's state, and the provider writes them after the subject; ids up to 120 characters are kept whole), and `store.touch(tabId, 'edited')` for edits. Markdown, Volume, Volumes and the Log Explorer report; Markdown's selected lines come from Live Preview and Source through `CollabSession.setSelection`. Selections may also carry `tab`, `part`, `node` (a key path or graph node id) and `field` (slice 43, the Forge editors). `workbench.subjectOpener` (`subjectOpeners`, id a subject type) opens a subject at a place; `src/workbench/links.ts` parses and opens `workbench:<type>/<id>?<where>` links, which `workbench.open {link}` and a click in a chat reply follow. Chat sends `store.snapshot('main', tabId)` (about 4 KB: references, labels and selections, never content; `previous` is the tab focused before the chat) while the conversation's Context control is on; the server's `workbench` provider renders it with every label quoted as data.
