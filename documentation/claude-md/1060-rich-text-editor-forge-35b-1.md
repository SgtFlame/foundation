**Rich text** (forge 35b-1, `documentation/MarkdownEditor.md` › *Built in forge 35b-1*): the rich-text editor is `frontend/common/src/editors/richtext/` (TipTap 2 MIT only, `y-prosemirror`), over a `CollabSession` made with `richTextContent` (`CollabSession` takes a `ContentFactory`; markdown's is the default); Volume document files open in it (kind `richtext`), and `doc.richText` and `record.fieldEditor` use it for a record's `richText` field, whose room holds `{format: 1, doc}` and saves with `record.setRichText`. Keep one ProseMirror: the workspace's `resolutions` and `foundation()`'s dedupe; a node a document may hold must be in `richTextSchema()`. Since forge 35b-2 `page` mode paginates with decorations (`pagination.ts`, `pages.ts`); y-prosemirror syncs no doc attrs, so what a document keeps there (page setup, headers and footers) lives in the room beside the fragment (`content.ts`). Since forge 35c-2 `blocks` mode (`doc.blocks`) adds the slash menu (app items, `command {id, block}` for a handler graph) and block moves as one transaction (`blockOps.ts`); block nodes are in `blockNodes.ts`.
