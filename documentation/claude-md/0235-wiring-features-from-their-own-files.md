**Wiring a feature from its own file** (the user, 2026-11-27): a new feature does not add lines to the shared lists in `serve.go` (the Contribute block, `connectorImpls`), apps/tools' `everything()` or forge's devtools catalog. It adds `backend/features/wiring/<feature>.go`, whose `init` calls `wiring.Register(wiring.Feature{Name, Contribute(reg, env), Adapter(o)})`: `Contribute` registers its contributions, reading the server only through `env` (`env.Connectors()`, `env.Connections()`, `env.RecordFiles()`, `env.Executable()`; add a field to `wiring.Env` when a feature needs more) and only inside the late-bound functions it hands to contributions; `Adapter` adds its in-process connector types, contracts and impls. Foundation's and forge's `serve` call `wiring.ContributeAll` (features in name order, after the built-in contributions and before `livetools`) and `wiring.Adapters`; the metadata test and forge's catalog call `wiring.ContributeAll(reg, nil)`. A contribution whose place among the built-in ones matters stays in `serve.go`. Calendar and Tasks are the examples. Where a shared list cannot be avoided, add one line in sorted order, never at the end.
