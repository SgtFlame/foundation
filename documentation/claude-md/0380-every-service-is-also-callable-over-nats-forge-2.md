Every service is also callable over NATS (forge 21, `documentation/Services.md`): after adding or changing an operation run `cli gen services` in `backend` (its generated `proxy/` and `server/` and `features/services/catalog`; `go test ./features/services/...` fails when they are stale), and mark an operation that only same-process Go code may call `//svc:local`, one that hands back a channel `//svc:stream`.
