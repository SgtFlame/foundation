**OAuth for connections** (forge 26a-1, `documentation/Connectors.md` › *Built in forge 26a-1*): a type with `OAuth {provider, scopes}` signs in through `connectors/oauth` (`connection.signIn` live call, the `/api/v1/connectors/oauth/callback` page, app registrations in `connectors.oauth`); its tokens are the connection's `oauth` secret, kept only by `connection.setTokens`, and adapters get `accessToken`/`expiresAt`, refreshed once across processes; test with `oauth/oauthtest`.
