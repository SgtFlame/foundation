- **Administration** - Tenants (`src/editors/tenants/`: members with their roles, *Roles…* opening the Share dialog on the current tenant) and Users (`src/editors/users/`, with the *Super Administrator* and *Full logging* switches), Roles (`src/editors/roles/`, the Role Editor: roles with their permission matrix and grants, custom tenant roles, installed apps' roles (*App roles*, read-only, with a table of their scoped permissions and row rules), and the Access checker; `ResourcePicker`) Moderation (`src/editors/moderation/`, the queue of global Chronicle shares) and Secrets (`src/editors/secrets/`, with `secret.list` on `app`: server secrets and the app scope's secret settings, each with *Set…* in a password dialog and *Delete*; never a value), each shown by permission through `canIn` (the Volumes command shows only in tenant `1`). `src/components/share/ShareDialog.tsx` (helpers in `grants.ts`) lists and edits the grants on one resource, with those inherited from above; the Volumes editor's *Share volume* opens it. Account (`src/editors/account/`, kind `account`) opens from the account menu. Log Explorer (`src/editors/logs/`, `new LogsObject('explorer')`): searches the stored logs over a time range with a structured builder (`builder.ts`: `toLogsQL`, and `fromLogsQL` for what it can show) or LogsQL in Monaco (`logsql.ts`: tokenizer, completion, parse-error markers); a level-stacked hits histogram (drag to zoom), virtualized `LogRow`s with quick filters, trace and surrounding-record links, stats as a table, and live tail. Every run writes a deep link (`/workbench?logs=<query>&range=1h`), which `Workbench.tsx` opens on load; other views open the Explorer with `openLogs(view)` (DevTools' Log tab does, for a record with a trace), and `logsOn(view)` gives one tab per query and range.
