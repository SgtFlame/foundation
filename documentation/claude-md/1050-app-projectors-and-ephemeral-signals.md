**App projectors and ephemeral signals** (forge 26d-1, `documentation/Apps.md` › *Built in forge 26d-1*): a manifest's `projectors` fold its own entity types' record events with a pure `projector` graph (*On fold*) into `app.<appId>.<name>`, read with *Read projection* and resolved per install and generation by `installs.ProjectorResolver` (`Projections.SetResolver`, set in `serve.go`); a manifest's `ephemeral` topics are core NATS subjects `eph.<tenant>.<app>.<topic>.<key>`, never stored, sent by *Publish* (`live.publish`) and followed by the `live.ephemeral` query or *Subscribe to signals*, both checked against the keying record's access.
