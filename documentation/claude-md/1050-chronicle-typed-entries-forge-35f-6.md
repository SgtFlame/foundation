**Typed Chronicle entries** (forge 35f-6, `documentation/Chronicle.md` › *Built in forge 35f-6*): a tenant's or project's entry types (`chronicle.TypeDef`, fields from the records' catalog, `CheckType`, starters in `chronicle.Starters`) resolved with `chronicle.TypeFor`; an entry's `Facts` (fields and typed links as text, `RefreshFacts` after every change) are what search reads; a project's entries (`Entry.Scope`, `project:<page>`) are read through `chronicle.VisibleTo(…, ReadableProjects(ctx, p))` and `Owner.Projects`, never `Visible` alone, in any new read of entries.
