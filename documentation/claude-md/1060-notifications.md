**Notifications** (forge 26e-1, `documentation/Notifications.md`): `features/notifications` keeps what a person must see as records of the tenant's `notification` type (only the recipient touches one; made by the service principal); send them with `notifications.Notify` or the *Notify* node (`notify.send`), always with a key (`<app>:<subject>:<kind>`) so a retry notifies once; read them with `notifications.mine` and `notifications.count`.
